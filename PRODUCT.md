# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Freelancers and small creative studios (designers, 3D/rendering artists, small agencies), many in the Gulf (AED, SAR pricing). They mostly use UnitPulse at a desk on a laptop during the working day, in longer sessions alongside email and client chat. A business owner runs it; staff members can be invited and assigned to projects.

## Product Purpose

UnitPulse stops free work. It records the agreed scope and price of a project, catches every client request that falls outside it, turns that request into a priced change order the client approves (and pays a deposit on) before the work starts, then tracks invoices, expenses and time so the owner knows what a project really earns. Success: no extra request goes unpriced, and the owner opens UnitPulse every working day because it is where client requests land.

## Positioning

The scope is the baseline. Every request is measured against it, so "is this extra?" has a recorded answer and a price, instead of an awkward conversation.

## Operating Context

Daily loop: a client message arrives (email, WhatsApp), the owner logs the request, decides if it is in scope, prices it as a change order, sends it for approval, collects the deposit, does the work, invoices. Clients approve and pay through a portal link, without an account. Calendar feeds export due dates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Clients, projects (scope: deliverables, exclusions, revision limit; pricing: hourly or fixed per request), change requests, change orders with approval and deposit, invoices (UnitPulse never holds money; Stripe optional), expenses, tasks and time, files, messages and updates, AI drafting of replies, reports on profit by project and client, calendar, activity log, client portal, staff with roles. Deletes are soft deletes. Next.js + Supabase.

## Brand Commitments

Name: UnitPulse. Keep the floating sidebar structure (the user asked for it to survive the redesign). Everything else visual is open. No gamification: no points, badges, streaks or levels unless the user asks.

## Evidence on Hand

Only sample data (fixtures). No customers, testimonials or metrics to quote.

## Product Principles

1. The extra request is the hero moment: catching and pricing one must be the fastest thing in the app.
2. Money is always shown with its currency and its state (owed, overdue, paid).
3. Every screen says what to do next; empty is an invitation, not a dead end.
4. Calm, honest pull instead of tricks: people return because their work lives here.
